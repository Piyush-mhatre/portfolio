Investment Recommender

Tell it your risk tolerance, liquidity needs, tax preference and time horizon, and a scikit-learn Decision Tree picks the best match from 14 Indian investment options, with three alternatives and a plain-English reason for each. Part of the Explainable AI Financial Advisor project — this is the live backend, not a mockup. Note: with only 14 options, the tree works like a nearest-match lookup, not a model trained on real market data.
